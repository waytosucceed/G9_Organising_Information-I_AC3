Key Takeaways


  1. What does it mean to organise information from broadest to narrowest?
    It means scaling ideas differently — some sentences speak broadly about many things at once, while others narrow down to one specific detail.

  2. How can we picture this organisation?
    We can picture it as an inverted pyramid, with the broad general idea at the top narrowing down into specific details below it.

  3. Why do writers organise information this way?
    We know that a broad idea could apply to many situations, while a narrow idea applies to just one, which helps writers show contrast or highlight something significant.

  4. How does this organisation help the reader?
    We know that it makes complex information easier to digest, helps us find particular information quickly, presents ideas in manageable chunks, and creates a logical flow.

  5. What signal words help us identify a general idea?
    General ideas use abstract nouns along with hedge words like "often," "always" and "usually."

  6. What signal words help us identify a specific idea?
    Specific ideas use named objects, exact physical actions and sensory detail — such as "oak limb," "crept," and "swaying" from the text we read.

  7. What is the difference between a general and a specific idea?
    We know that a general idea covers a wide range of situations using broad terms, while a specific idea uses exact names, actions and details to describe just one.


Words that I Learnt from the Story 📚

Aver
Meaning: To assert or affirm with confidence; to declare positively.
Example: The witness firmly averred that she had seen the suspect near the scene of the crime.

Indurate
Meaning: To harden physically or emotionally; to make callous or resistant.
Example: Years of hardship had indurated his spirit, leaving him unmoved by suffering.

August
Meaning: Inspiring reverence or admiration; majestic, dignified, or venerable.
Example: The cathedral’s august architecture commanded awe from all who entered.
